[Muumuu Domain](/en/articles/muumuu-domain) and [Onamae.com](/en/articles/onamae) are both Japanese domain services that come up as candidates when an individual or a small business registers a custom domain. Laying the two dissections on top of each other, the two are competitors and, at the same time, stand upstream and downstream of each other, since Muumuu Domain uses Onamae.com as a registrar. The differences show less in the number of TLDs they handle than in how each sets its first-year discount, how it presents renewal fees and the adjustment fee, and what it must be contracted with for domains to become free.

This article was put together by rereading both services' official pages on October 11, 2026. This site has not registered domains with both to compare how fast procedures go or how support responds, so it does not rank how they feel in use. All prices include tax and are per year unless noted otherwise.

## Company: an ICANN-accredited registrar, and a registration agent that uses it

:::fact
According to the history of GMO Internet's former company (as of 2026-10-11), Onamae.com started in September 1999 as "Discount Domain Dot Com." It is now run by GMO Internet, Inc. (TSE Prime, securities code 4784), and the "About Onamae.com" page describes it as an ICANN-accredited registrar, "one of only 19 registrars accredited in Japan," with more than 630 TLDs and more than 49 million cumulative registrations. According to GMO Pepabo's history, Muumuu Domain was launched in January 2004 by the company (then paperboy&co. Ltd., a limited company) as a domain registration agency service. It is now run by GMO Pepabo, Inc. (Standard Market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange, securities code 3633). Its terms of service define the service as the "Muumuu Domain domain registration agency service," state that it uses Onamae.com (GMO Internet, Inc.) and eNom, Inc. of the United States as registrars, and add that other registrars may be used for some domains. Muumuu Domain's home page claims 5.6 million total registrations and more than 620 TLDs. Both companies' overviews list their major shareholder as "GMO Internet Group, Inc. and others."
:::

| Item | Muumuu Domain | Onamae.com |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Started | January 2004 | September 1999 (as "Discount Domain Dot Com") |
| Operator | GMO Pepabo, Inc. (TSE Standard) | GMO Internet, Inc. (TSE Prime) |
| Role | Domain registration agent (registrars: Onamae.com, eNom of the U.S. and others) | ICANN-accredited registrar |
| Headline figures | 620+ TLDs, 5.6 million total registrations | 630+ TLDs, more than 49 million cumulative registrations |

:::pull
One of them uses the other as its registrar. Even so, the first-year price tags, renewal fees and conditions for free domains are decided separately.
:::

## Price: a 1-yen entrance, and 750 yen with no end date

:::fact
According to Onamae.com's price list (checked 2026-10-11), .com and .net registrations cost 1 yen, "one per person," and 750 yen from the second one; romanized .jp costs 1 yen (1,860 yen from the second), and 24 items are offered for 1 yen on the same terms. Registering .co.jp costs 930 yen. According to the renewal pricing page, a one-year renewal for holders of four or fewer domains costs 1,408 yen for .com, 1,628 yen for .net, 3,124 yen for .jp and 7,678 yen for .co.jp, falling in three steps for holders of five or more, 11 or more and 31 or more; at 31 or more, .com costs 1,287 yen and .co.jp 4,125 yen. According to the price list, transfers cost 1,012 yen for .com and .net, 3,124 yen for .jp and 1,320 yen for .co.jp. According to Muumuu Domain's price list (checked the same day), .com costs 750 yen to register and 1,728 yen to renew or transfer; .net costs 1,848 yen for all three; .jp costs 990 yen to register and 3,344 yen to renew or transfer; and .co.jp costs 900 yen to register and 4,378 yen to renew (no transfer price in the table). The price list shows no end date for registration prices, and the September 8, 2026 news post that sets .co.jp at 900 yen says the price applies only to new one-year registrations and may end without notice. Muumuu Domain's price list shows no renewal tiers by number of domains held.
:::

| .com renewal | 1,728 yen | 1,408 yen (four or fewer held; 1,287 yen at 31 or more) |

:::guess
Looking only at the first year, Onamae.com's 1 yen stands out, but it is limited to one per person, and from the second .com the price is the same 750 yen as at Muumuu Domain. Renewals of .com, .net and .jp are 220 to 320 yen cheaper at Onamae.com, while .co.jp is 3,300 yen cheaper at Muumuu Domain. Since Muumuu Domain's terms of service name Onamae.com as a registrar, Muumuu Domain's higher renewal prices for .com and others appear to include its share as a registration agent. However, which registrar is used for which domain is not published, and the wholesale prices are unknown. That only .co.jp is reversed reads as a result of Onamae.com pricing .co.jp renewals to fall steeply with the number of domains held, and setting the base price for four or fewer high.
:::

## Adjustment fee: the same rates, different switch times

:::fact
According to Muumuu Domain's December 26, 2022 news post, the company introduced a "service maintenance adjustment fee" of about 10% to 20% on bills for new registrations, renewals and transfers from 00:00 on February 1, 2023, citing soaring domestic electricity prices, the weakening yen and higher wholesale prices for domains, and planned to review it every one to three months. Onamae.com's September 4, 2026 news post says that, "in view of rising prices of various kinds," it temporarily charges a fixed percentage on the applicable fees from February 1, 2023. For 2026, Muumuu Domain's news posts set the rate at 27.00% for August, 26.25% for September and 25.50% for October, each switching at 00:00 on the 1st of the month and applied to the tax-excluded domain price. Onamae.com's news posts set the rate for domains (including options) at 27.00% from 12:00 on August 3, 26.25% from 12:00 on September 1 and 25.50% from 12:00 on October 1, each citing changes in exchange rates as the main factor, and say that bills incurred between 00:00 and 12:00 on the day of a change are calculated at the previous rate. In other words, from 00:00 to 12:00 on October 1, the rate was 25.50% at Muumuu Domain and 26.25% at Onamae.com. In this site's own observation (2026-10-11), both services' price tables load with the checkbox for including the fee (Muumuu Domain's "include the service maintenance adjustment fee," Onamae.com's "adjustment fee included") unchecked, and show amounts without the fee. A one-year .com renewal including the fee is 2,168 yen according to the data embedded in Muumuu Domain's price list page, and 1,767 yen at Onamae.com by this site's calculation of adding 25.50% to the 1,408 yen for holders of four or fewer.
:::

| Service maintenance adjustment fee | Muumuu Domain | Onamae.com |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduced | From 00:00 on February 1, 2023 (planned at about 10–20% at first) | From February 1, 2023 |
| August 2026 | 27.00% (from 00:00 on August 1) | 27.00% (from 12:00 on August 3) |
| September 2026 | 26.25% (from 00:00 on September 1) | 26.25% (from 12:00 on September 1) |
| October 2026 | 25.50% (from 00:00 on October 1) | 25.50% (from 12:00 on October 1) |
| Reason given in news posts | Changes in exchange rates, etc. (at introduction: electricity prices, weak yen, higher wholesale prices) | Main factor: changes in exchange rates (introduction: "rising prices of various kinds") |
| Price list default view | Fee not included | Fee not included |
| .com renewal (fee included) | 2,168 yen | 1,767 yen (this site's calculation) |

:::guess
The two services' rates moved in step from August to October 2026. Combined with the fact that Muumuu Domain's terms name Onamae.com as a registrar, Muumuu Domain appears to pass the rate set by the upstream company almost directly into its customers' prices. The switch times differ, however: Muumuu Domain switches at 00:00 on the 1st of each month and Onamae.com at 12:00, and in August the gap was two and a half days. In months when the rate falls, as in September and October, Muumuu Domain has the lower rate for the 12 hours until Onamae.com switches. Because both price lists show amounts without the fee by default, a comparison that lines up only the numbers in the tables needs care, since a gap of about a quarter remains between them and what is actually paid.
:::

## Conditions for free domains: a server or Google Workspace, or a server only

:::fact
According to Muumuu Domain's "domains free forever" page (checked 2026-10-11), contracting a domain together with Muumuu Server or Google Workspace (Standard plan or higher) makes the registration and renewal fees of eligible domains 0 yen. The page says there are "more than 45" eligible TLDs, and its list shows 48, including .jp, .com, .co.jp and .net (this site's count). The conditions are registering with a credit card, a one-year contract and contracting and continuing to use an eligible option at the same time; the perk ends if the option is cancelled or not paid for, and if both options were contracted, it continues as long as either one does. Premium domains are excluded. Muumuu Server costs 1,430 yen a month, and the first two months are 0 yen only when it is contracted together with a domain. According to Onamae.com's page for registering a domain together with a rental server, applying together with a rental server makes domain registration 1 yen, and free renewal covers up to two domains. The first can be chosen from 24 eligible TLDs such as .com, .jp and .net, and its renewal is 0 yen while the server is in use (if the server is cancelled within 12 months after a free renewal, the actual renewal cost is charged). The second is a .com registered within the validity period of a coupon worth the .com registration price that arrives the following month and added to the server. Free renewal ends when the server is cancelled or when the eligible domain is moved to a different Onamae ID, and domains designated as registry premium domains are excluded. .co.jp is not in the eligible list. The RS rental server plan costs 2,398 yen a month, with the first month's fee and the setup fee free.
:::

:::fact
Protective options are priced differently too. Article 27 of Muumuu Domain's terms of service lets users publish GMO Pepabo's information in the Whois record in their place free of charge, within the limits set by the upstream registrars and registries, at registration or from the members' page, and says registrar transfers are not possible while the proxy is applied. Onamae.com's Whois privacy costs 1,078 yen a year per domain and is free when ordered together with a new registration. As for options that put an approval step in front of setting changes, Muumuu Domain's domain lock costs 1,320 yen a year per domain and makes changes to four items, Whois information, name servers, changes of administrator and contract settings, subject to a request and an approval. Onamae.com's Domain Protection costs 1,078 yen a year per domain and makes procedures such as name server changes, the domain transfer lock, auto-renewal settings and Whois information changes subject to the registrant's approval.
:::

| Item | Muumuu Domain | Onamae.com |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What renewals must be bundled with | Muumuu Server or Google Workspace (Standard plan or higher) | A rental server |
| What becomes free | Registration and renewal | Renewal (registration is 1 yen) |
| Eligible domains | More than 45 (48 in the list, including .co.jp) | Up to two: the first from 24 TLDs, the second .com only (.co.jp not included) |
| Monthly price of the bundled service | Muumuu Server 1,430 yen (first two months 0 yen when contracted together) | RS plan 2,398 yen (first month's fee and setup fee free) |
| Whois proxy | Free | 1,078 yen a year (free with a new registration) |
| Option making setting changes subject to approval | Domain lock, 1,320 yen a year | Domain Protection, 1,078 yen a year |

:::guess
Both are mechanisms that switch a contract from collecting a standalone domain renewal fee to collecting a server's monthly fee. Muumuu Domain also accepts Google Workspace and includes .co.jp, which appears designed to keep businesses that want to set up a corporate domain and email together, even without a server. Onamae.com limits free renewal to two domains and the second to a .com taken with the following month's coupon, which reads as limiting free domains to those used with the server. For protective options, Muumuu Domain is cheaper for the Whois proxy, and Onamae.com is cheaper for the approval-based option.
:::

## Entry points for AI agents: an MCP server already open, and one announced

:::fact
According to GMO Pepabo's press release of October 7, 2026, Muumuu Domain started offering a remote MCP server on March 31, 2026, "the first among domestic domain registration services," made "Muumuu Domain API v2" generally available in May, and added an AI assistant, "Muumuu Domain Concierge," to its control panel in June. Since September 30, the remote MCP server has been listed as a "verified" connector in the "Connectors Directory" of Anthropic's Claude, and payment for domain purchases is confirmed by users themselves on Muumuu Domain's site. The MCP page claims "Japan's first domain MCP server," covers everything from domain search and registration to DNS settings with 11 tools, and says the MCP server itself is free to use while registering and renewing domains costs the regular prices. According to Onamae.com's reseller program page, automating domain registration, renewal, information changes and other procedures through the API is limited to businesses with an Advanced Partner contract; the API specification is handed over after a non-disclosure agreement is signed, and a 100,000-yen deposit is charged when a partner contract is completed. According to a May 22, 2026 news post, Onamae.com plans to offer a "REST API" for "Onamae.com Rental Server Basic" that lets external applications handle server information, domains, subdomains, free SSL, DNS records, mail accounts and WordPress settings around October 2026, and also plans an "MCP server" for connecting with AI services, with the timing to be announced later. The headlines in Onamae.com's list of 2026 news posts (262 items), checked by this site on October 11, 2026, included none announcing the launch of an API or MCP server.
:::

| Item | Muumuu Domain | Onamae.com |
| --- | --- | --- |
| API for operating domains | API v2 (generally available in May 2026) | For businesses with an Advanced Partner contract (specification after an NDA) |
| MCP server | Remote MCP server for domains (since March 31, 2026; free to use; a "verified" Claude connector since September 30) | Planned for Rental Server Basic (timing to be announced) |

:::guess
Muumuu Domain was first to open everything from domain search to purchase in a form ordinary users can reach from AI agents. Onamae.com's domain API is a tool for businesses, with the specification handed over after an NDA, and its connection with AI has been announced first on the rental server side. Within the same group, this appears to be a division of roles in which Muumuu Domain, the registration agent, tries new ways of buying first, while Onamae.com, the registrar, opens up from wholesale for businesses and from operating its own servers. Muumuu Domain's design of having purchases made through MCP confirmed on its site is meant to prevent charges from being made on an AI's judgment alone, and the same question will presumably come up when Onamae.com opens domain purchases to AI.
:::

## Referral programs: Muumuu Domain publishes a reward table, Onamae.com leaves it to the ASPs

:::fact
Muumuu Domain's affiliate page (checked 2026-10-11) publishes a reward table: 5,000 yen for a new Muumuu Server contract (three months or longer), 3,000 yen for a Muumuu Domain for WP Hosting contract, 2,000 yen per account for a Google Workspace contract, 100 yen each for a domain registration and a domain transfer to Muumuu Domain (50 yen for transfers of some domains), and 1 yen per ad click. The same page recommends that first-time affiliates register with A8.net for free. Onamae.com's affiliate page explains that affiliates register for free with either A8.net or ValueCommerce, apply for the Onamae.com program from that network's dashboard, and usually receive an approval email within one to two business days, but it does not list reward amounts.
:::

| Item | Muumuu Domain | Onamae.com |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASP | A8.net (the page recommends registering) | A8.net or ValueCommerce |
| Published rewards | Muumuu Server 5,000 yen, WP Hosting 3,000 yen, Google Workspace 2,000 yen, domain registration 100 yen and more | Not listed on the page |

:::guess
In Muumuu Domain's reward table, a new Muumuu Server contract pays 5,000 yen against 100 yen for a domain registration, a 50-fold gap. The design of wanting referring writers to recommend the monthly contracts on top of the domain rather than the domain itself appears to show directly in the reward amounts. When reading a referral article about either service, it is worth assuming the writer may be paid and checking not just the first-year price but also renewal fees and the adjustment fee.
:::

## One shared tech stack item

The tech stack comparison shown below this page is a mechanical match of the two articles' techStack entries. Only one item, Cloudflare, was judged shared, and it sits in different places. In this site's own observation (2026-10-11), Onamae.com's official site itself is behind Cloudflare and returned challenges at the entry points for login, Whois lookup and renewal. At Muumuu Domain, a Cloudflare response came back from the help center running on Zendesk (support.muumuu-domain.com), while the main site's pages returned server: istio-envoy and were routed to three applications in PHP, Ruby and Nuxt. Also, Muumuu Domain's techStack lists Onamae.com as an upstream registrar, but because the names are written differently, the mechanical match does not count it as shared.

The question for those choosing comes down to how many domains they will hold, together with what, and for how long. For someone who wants to start the first domain for 1 yen, will keep .com or .jp for years, or manages dozens of domains together, Onamae.com, with the lower first-year and renewal prices, is the candidate. For someone who holds .co.jp, uses Google Workspace alongside, wants to add the Whois proxy for free even after registration, or wants to search for and register domains from AI agents, Muumuu Domain is the candidate. What separates two services in the same group, one of which uses the other as its registrar, is not where the domains come from but the design of how they sell: which price to put at the entrance, what to bundle for domains to become free, and to whom to open the API.
