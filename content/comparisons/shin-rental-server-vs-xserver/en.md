[Shin Rental Server](/en/articles/shin-rental-server) and [Xserver](/en/articles/xserver) are both shared hosting services sold by XServer Inc. for WordPress blogs and small business websites. Two products of the same company sit on the same comparison shelf. Laying the two dissections on top of each other, the difference shows less in the performance of the infrastructure than in how each promises the same CPU and memory tiers, how disk and price are shifted, and who is asked to refer customers.

This article was put together by rereading both services' official pages on October 11, 2026. This site has not signed up for both under the same conditions to measure page speed or stability, so it does not rank how they feel in use. All prices include tax and are monthly equivalents when the full contract length is paid up front.

## Company: one company, two brands

:::fact
According to XServer Inc.'s company overview and history (as of 2026-10-11), the company was founded on January 23, 2004, is headquartered at Grand Front Osaka in Kita Ward, Osaka, and has capital of 100 million yen (including capital reserve). The Xserver shared hosting service started in July 2003 under the trade name Xserver. According to the same history, the company launched the new-generation hosting service "wpX Shin Rental Server" (now "Shin Rental Server") in May 2021. According to a Shin Rental Server announcement (2025-09-02), the services offered under the Shin account were transferred to group company XServer Inc. on October 1, 2025, as part of a group reorganization. Xserver's home page claims the No. 1 domestic share (per W3Techs as of June 2026, according to its notes), 260,000 companies and 2.5 million sites in operation; the notes give the company count as of September 2026 and the site count as of September 2025, both as totals for Xserver and the business service XServer Business. Shin Rental Server's home page claims to be "No. 1 fastest in Japan" and says there is no restriction on the genre of content that can be run.
:::

| Item | Shin Rental Server | Xserver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Started | May 2021 (as "wpX Shin Rental Server") | July 2003 |
| Operator | XServer Inc. (transferred October 1, 2025) | XServer Inc. |
| Regular plan names | Basic, Standard, Premium | Standard, Premium, Business |
| Home page banner | "No. 1 fastest in Japan," no restriction on content genre | No. 1 domestic share, 260,000 companies, 2.5 million sites in operation |

:::pull
The CPU and memory tiers carry the same numbers. The difference is whether those numbers are promised, and how disk and price are shifted.
:::

## Resources and infrastructure: the same tiers, guaranteed or allocated

:::fact
Xserver's server specification page (checked 2026-10-11) gives "resource guarantee" values by server: on servers from sv13001 onward, Standard gets 6 virtual cores and 8 GB of memory, Premium 8 cores and 12 GB, and Business 10 cores and 16 GB (on servers sv10001 to sv13000 the values are a step lower, such as 4 cores and 4 GB for Standard). Shin Rental Server's feature list describes the vCPUs and memory of its regular plans as the cores and capacity "allocated to each server account": 6 cores and 8 GB for Basic, 8 cores and 12 GB for Standard, and 10 cores and 16 GB for Premium. Shin's business plan page says that, unlike the regular plans, the business plans guarantee resource values per plan, and gives Business Basic a guarantee of 4 cores and 4 GB. For CPUs, Xserver's home page says it uses 5th-generation AMD EPYC, and Shin's features page says it uses 4th-generation AMD EPYC. Shin's home page says it actively adopts the latest technology, and the page's description (og:description) says it is built on Xserver's system and applies Xserver's system enhancements ahead of it, as fast as possible. Disk is 500 GB, 600 GB and 700 GB on Xserver's Standard, Premium and Business, and 700 GB, 1,000 GB and 1,200 GB on Shin's Basic, Standard and Premium, all NVMe. For transfer, Xserver's server specification page says it is unlimited (with restrictions when an excessive load is placed on the network or servers), while Shin's server specification page gives daily guidelines of 900 GB for Basic, 1,200 GB for Standard and 1,500 GB for Premium and says speed limits or other restrictions may apply when usage greatly exceeds them. For automatic backups, both feature lists say they keep 14 days of web and mail data and of MySQL.
:::

| Item | Shin Rental Server | Xserver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier with 6 cores and 8 GB | Basic (allocated) | Standard (resource guarantee on sv13001 onward) |
| Resource guarantee | business plans only (Business Basic: 4 cores, 4 GB) | guarantee values stated for regular plans (they vary by server number) |
| CPU | 4th-generation AMD EPYC | 5th-generation AMD EPYC |
| Disk (lowest tier first) | 700 GB, 1,000 GB, 1,200 GB | 500 GB, 600 GB, 700 GB |
| Transfer | daily guidelines of 900 GB, 1,200 GB and 1,500 GB (restrictions if greatly exceeded) | unlimited (restrictions under excessive load) |
| Automatic backups | 14 days | 14 days |

:::guess
The same figure of 6 cores and 8 GB is written as a guarantee by Xserver and as an allocation by Shin, which moves the guarantee to its separately priced business plans. Shin raising the price of the same tier slightly and adding a lot of disk, without guaranteeing CPU and memory, appears to split customers along plan lines: those who value capacity, and companies that pay for stability. The CPU generation written on the pages is, contrary to the brand messages, newer for the main brand, Xserver. What Shin's description says it applies first is system enhancements, and the hardware generation presumably follows a different schedule; but which servers accounts are placed on is not published, so the pages alone cannot settle which is actually newer.
:::

## Price: Shin's regular price is slightly higher, and on October 11 only Xserver was discounted

:::fact
According to Shin Rental Server's pricing page (checked 2026-10-11), the monthly price on a 36-month contract is 1,078 yen for Basic, 2,002 yen for Standard and 4,004 yen for Premium, and the new-contract and renewal tables show the same amounts. According to its news index, six campaigns ran from December 26, 2025 to October 7, 2026, and from the second on, each was announced on the day the previous one ended; the page for the last one, "up to half-price cashback," said it had ended. According to Xserver's pricing page (checked the same day), the regular monthly price on a 36-month contract is 990 yen for Standard, 1,980 yen for Premium and 3,960 yen for Business, and campaign prices of 693 yen, 1,386 yen and 2,772 yen applied from 17:00 on October 5 to 17:00 on November 4, 2026. On 12-month contracts, the regular 1,100, 2,200 and 4,400 yen became 990, 1,980 and 3,960 yen. Both require the full contract length to be paid up front, and neither charges a setup fee.
:::

| 36-month contract (tax included, monthly equivalent) | Shin Rental Server | Xserver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier with 6 cores and 8 GB | Basic 1,078 yen | Standard 990 yen (693 yen in the October 11 campaign) |
| Tier with 8 cores and 12 GB | Standard 2,002 yen | Premium 1,980 yen (campaign: 1,386 yen) |
| Tier with 10 cores and 16 GB | Premium 4,004 yen | Business 3,960 yen (campaign: 2,772 yen) |
| Difference in regular price | 22 to 88 yen higher | — |
| Campaign on October 11, 2026 | none (the previous one ended October 7) | up to 30% off (until 17:00 on November 4) |

:::guess
Lined up at regular prices, Shin looks like the same tier with more disk for 22 to 88 yen more. But Shin has kept campaigns running almost without a break for more than nine months, and October 11 happened to be a day when only one of the two was discounted. Since the presence and depth of a campaign can flip which one is cheaper in practice, the reliable way to compare appears to be checking both pricing pages on the day you sign up.
:::

## Trials and perks: the same terms, slightly different free perks

:::fact
Both services' terms of service (checked 2026-10-11) set a trial period of 10 days from the notice that service has started, start the full contract on the day payment is confirmed during the trial, and, in the cancellation clause, state that fees already paid are not refunded. The wording of the clauses is almost the same. For free domains, according to Xserver's pricing page, new Standard contracts get one with a 12-month contract and two with 24 months or longer, and Premium and Business get two with any contract. Shin's support manual says one domain from a designated list is free on the regular plans and two on the business plans (the features page says "any two," so the pages are not consistent). The paid WordPress theme "Xwrite" (regularly 9,900 yen a year) is free on Premium and Business at Xserver according to its pricing page (not on Standard), and free on every plan except Basic at Shin.
:::

| Item | Shin Rental Server | Xserver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free trial | 10 days | 10 days |
| Refund of paid fees | none (terms of service) | none (terms of service) |
| Free domain | one on regular plans (two on business plans) | Standard: one with 12 months, two with 24 months or longer; Premium and Business: two |
| Xwrite (regularly 9,900 yen a year) | free except on Basic | free on Premium and Business |

## Entry points for AI agents: the same parts, six days apart, and a remote version on one side

:::fact
According to Xserver's official news, XServer API launched on April 16, 2026, and the MCP server and CLI on May 12. According to Shin Rental Server's news, the "Shin Account API" launched on April 22, and its MCP server and CLI on the same May 12. Xserver also has a remote version of its MCP server (endpoint `https://api.xserver.ne.jp/mcp`) that needs no MCP server installed on the device and no API key: an AI agent logs in to the XServer account and the user only approves the scope of access, and the manual gives the steps to add it as a Claude connector. Shin Rental Server's MCP Server manual describes running it on the user's own PC as `npx -y shincloud-mcp`, which requires Node.js 18 or later and an API key.
:::

:::guess
Releasing the API six days later and the MCP server and CLI on the same day appears to be possible because the two services share the same development setup. Putting the remote MCP version on the main brand first reads as choosing to open AI-driven operation to customers who do not use a terminal on Xserver, the service with more users.
:::

## Referral programs: the main brand pays more, Shin runs its own affiliate program

:::fact
According to Xserver's affiliate page (checked 2026-10-11), its A8.net rewards are 5,000 yen for Standard, 7,500 yen for Premium and 10,000 yen for Business. According to its refer-a-friend page, the referrer receives up to 20% of the friend's first fee and the referred friend gets up to 20% off the first fee, and the program can be combined with campaigns. According to Shin Rental Server's affiliate page, its ASPs are A8.net and Moshimo Affiliate, with rewards of 3,000 yen for Basic, 5,000 yen for Standard and 8,000 yen for Premium. Its in-house Shin Cloud Affiliate pays 3,500 to 9,000 yen for referred contracts of six months or longer, gives the referred customer 10% off the first fee, and cannot be combined with A8.net or Moshimo Affiliate.
:::

| Item | Shin Rental Server | Xserver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASP rewards (lowest tier first) | 3,000, 5,000 and 8,000 yen (A8.net, Moshimo Affiliate) | 5,000, 7,500 and 10,000 yen (A8.net) |
| In-house referral program | Shin Cloud Affiliate (3,500 to 9,000 yen; 10% off the referred customer's first fee) | Refer-a-friend program (up to 20% of the first fee for both sides) |

:::guess
On reward amounts alone, a referring writer earns more per sale with Xserver. Shin paying more than its ASP rates through its own affiliate program, and banning combination with the ASPs, appears designed to pass the ASP commission on to referrers and draw writers to its own channel. When reading a recommendation of either, it is worth assuming the writer may be paid and checking the regular price and the campaign deadline.
:::

## Six shared technologies

The tech stack comparison shown below this page mechanically matches the techStack of the two articles. The six technologies judged to be shared are nginx, Apache 2.4, AMD EPYC, all-NVMe RAID10, MariaDB 10.11 and XPageSpeed. AMD EPYC counts as shared because the match is by name; as shown above, the generations differ, 4th and 5th. The APIs and MCP servers appear on one side only because of the different names, Shin Cloud and XServer, though their release dates, key format and list of available operations are nearly the same.

The question for the buyer splits by what they want to pay for. If you want more disk at the same price tier, keep many photos or videos, or can time your sign-up to a campaign, Shin Rental Server is a candidate. If you want CPU and memory values written as a guarantee, prefer servers with the newer CPU generation, want to start cheaply in the current campaign, or are a writer choosing by referral rewards, Xserver is a candidate. What separates the same company's two brands is not the quality of the infrastructure but the design of how they sell: which numbers are promised, and which numbers go on the price tag.
