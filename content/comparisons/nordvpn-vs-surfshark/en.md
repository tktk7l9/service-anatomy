[NordVPN](/en/articles/nordvpn) and [Surfshark](/en/articles/surfshark) both have Japanese sites or help pages, both sell a VPN on long prepaid terms, and both point to Deloitte assurance. Overlaying the two anatomies, the differences that matter when choosing appear to lie in the amount after renewal, the number of devices, who the contract is with, and how much can be checked from outside.

This comparison reads both companies' official pages on November 2, 2026. One thing cannot be compared. `nordvpn.com` refused our requests with a Cloudflare challenge, so we have not read NordVPN's first-term prices on its pricing page or its plan comparison table. We did not subscribe to both and measure speed, and speed is out of scope. Amounts state their tax treatment and are lined up excluding tax.

## Companies: one group, described as run separately

:::fact
NordVPN's General Terms of Service (updated August 10, 2026) name nordvpn S.A. of Panama as the contracting party, under Panamanian law. Payments by residents of Japan are said to be mainly processed by Nord Security JP Co., Ltd in Shibuya. Surfshark's terms name Surfshark B.V., incorporated in the Netherlands, with the law of the British Virgin Islands applying. Both say mandatory provisions of the country of residence may take precedence. Nord Security's announcement of September 28, 2023 says it "formed a group of companies" with Surfshark in 2022. Surfshark's About page says the two merged under one holding company in 2022 and operate as independent brands, and Surfshark's official blog of 2022 said they would continue as autonomous companies with separate infrastructure and product development plans.
:::

| Item | NordVPN | Surfshark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contracting party | nordvpn S.A. (Panama) | Surfshark B.V. (Netherlands) |
| Governing law | Panamanian law | Law of the British Virgin Islands |
| Origin | 2012, Lithuania | 2018, Lithuania |
| Company for payments in Japan | Nord Security JP Co., Ltd (Shibuya) named as main processor | No Japanese company in the terms we read |

:::pull
They can be chosen as separate brands, but they belong to the same group. Switching between these two may not achieve the aim of leaving an operator's capital behind.
:::

## Pricing: the renewal gap, and what could be read

:::fact
NordVPN's renewal price page (updated June 10, 2026) gives one-year renewal amounts for Japan of 21,960 yen for Basic, 23,640 yen for Standard, 28,320 yen for Plus, 34,680 yen for Complete, 42,720 yen for Ultra and 45,960 yen for Complete Max (tax extra). In the data embedded in the HTML of Surfshark's pricing page (fetched 2026-11-02), the renewal field reads 12,451 yen for Starter, 15,603 yen for One and 18,755 yen for One+ (excluding tax), and these amounts do not appear in the body of the pricing page. That day, under a "Black Friday" label, Surfshark's 24-month plans with 3 added months came to 8,046 yen for 27 months of Starter or One and 16,146 yen for One+ (excluding tax). NordVPN's first-term prices on its web pricing page could not be read. The Japanese App Store lists 12-month plans at 11,800 and 9,300 yen and 1-month plans at 2,100 and 1,350 yen, without explaining the conditions. Surfshark's 1-month Starter plan is 2,898 yen on the web. Both say they send a notice 30 days before renewal (except 1-month plans), and both say the displayed renewal prices may be subject to A/B price testing.
:::

| Excl. tax | NordVPN (Basic) | Surfshark (Starter) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-year renewal | 21,960 yen (1,830 yen a month) | 12,451 yen (about 1,038 yen a month) |
| One-year renewal, top tier | Complete Max 45,960 yen | One+ 18,755 yen |
| First term of the long plan (web) | Not read | 8,046 yen for 27 months |
| Where the renewal amount sits | Fixed per-country table (legal page) | A script-drawn table on another page, and data embedded in the pricing page |
| Money-back guarantee | 30 days from first purchase | 30 days from first purchase |

Monthly figures are our calculation. Surfshark's renewal amounts are values we read from the embedded data and can change with the time of fetching. NordVPN's App Store amounts are not in the table because the page does not state their tax treatment.

:::guess
From what could be read, Surfshark costs less at the stage of paying year after year, and the gap between Basic and Starter comes to 9,509 yen a year (our calculation). But the contents differ and the tier names do not line up. On the other hand, NordVPN's fixed table appears easier to verify. Surfshark's amount has moved with the time of fetching, so the reliable moment for a user to learn it is likely to be the pre-renewal email and the account screen.
:::

## Devices and use from Japan

:::fact
NordVPN's help center allows up to 10 devices at once on one subscription and lists dedicated IP locations in 31 countries and regions including Tokyo (our count). The free trial runs 7 days in 15 countries including Japan, for 1-year and 2-year Prime, Ultimate Max, Ultra and Complete Max plans. Surfshark's pricing FAQ promises unlimited simultaneous connections (3 during the free trial), and the fair use policy in its terms reserves the right to limit an excessive number of devices. Its Japan server page gives 80 servers in Tokyo and more than 4,500 servers in 100 countries worldwide. For NordVPN, the latest total we could read is more than 7,600 servers in 118 countries, from a May 2025 announcement, and we could not read the number in Japan. Surfshark's dedicated IP page lists Tokyo among 15 locations in the Japanese version and says 20 locations elsewhere in its text.
:::

:::guess
A household with more than 10 devices fits more simply on Surfshark. If 10 is enough, this difference is unlikely to decide the matter. Both offer a dedicated IP in Tokyo, though NordVPN's list is laid out consistently by city. For anyone who wants to confirm the number of servers in Japan, Surfshark's page is more specific.
:::

## Logging and outside review

:::fact
The NordVPN privacy notice (version of July 20, 2026) says internet activity is not logged, last session information is deleted automatically within 15 minutes after a session ends, and information on whether the service was used in the last 90 days is processed. Surfshark's privacy policy (updated October 12, 2026) says user ID, IP address and connection timestamps are deleted automatically within 15 minutes after a session ends. On assurance, NordVPN commissioned its fifth engagement from Deloitte Audit Lithuania after 2018, 2020, 2022 and 2023 (announced February 18, 2025, ISAE 3000), and Surfshark's official blog of June 16, 2025 reports another Deloitte assurance (the detailed ISAE 3000 report can be read in the account). NordVPN's report can also be read by logged-in users. We have read neither in full.
:::

:::fact
On published code, the NordSecurity organization on GitHub publishes the Linux client `nordvpn-linux` and the networking library `libtelio` under GPL-3.0. Surfshark's trust center lists 100% RAM servers and its audits. On protocols, NordVPN has NordLynx built on WireGuard, NordWhisper for restricted networks, OpenVPN and others, and post-quantum encryption can be turned on in settings for NordLynx in all apps. Surfshark has its own Dausos (currently in the macOS app), WireGuard, IKEv2 and OpenVPN.
:::

:::guess
Both no-logs claims are best taken as each company's own description plus the result of an assurance procedure on that description. The difference appears to be how each builds trust: NordVPN through the number of assessments and published client code, Surfshark through how it presents its server design and list of outside reviews. Because we have not read `nordvpn.com`, NordVPN's server design is not compared here.
:::

## Referrals: both need permission to post discounts

:::fact
Surfshark's affiliate page gives a revenue share starting at 40% of new sales, a 30-day cookie and a $100 minimum payout. NordVPN's affiliate terms set a 30-day cookie, a $100 minimum payout and cancellation on refunds or chargebacks, and the help center says the commission depends on the plan (we could not read the page with the rates). Both sets of terms forbid bidding on brand terms and posting refunds, credits or discounts without prior written permission. Pop-up ads are forbidden at NordVPN, and at Surfshark forbidden unless permitted in writing.
:::

:::guess
The skeleton of the two sets of terms is similar, which could reflect the shared group or simply standard industry wording. Readers of referral articles should keep in mind that both companies pay referrers and manage how discounts are shown.
:::

## Tech stack overlap

The tech stack comparison below this page is a mechanical match of the two articles' techStack entries. How each shared item is used is covered in each article's tech stack section.

The choice can probably be split like this. Surfshark, if you want a lower yearly cost over time, have more than 10 devices, or want to start with scam protection and similar tools added cheaply to the VPN. NordVPN, if you want to check the renewal amount in a fixed table first, want a client with public code on Linux, want to pick from a dedicated IP list including Tokyo, or want to try OS-level protection such as Next-gen antivirus. We could not compare NordVPN's first-term price, so before signing up, check all three on the pricing page and the checkout screen: the first-term total, the renewal amount and the tax. Whichever you choose, remember that it is a choice within one group.
