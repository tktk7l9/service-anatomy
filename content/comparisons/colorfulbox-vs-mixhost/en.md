[ColorfulBox](/en/articles/colorfulbox) and [mixhost](/en/articles/mixhost) are Japanese shared hosts for WordPress blogs and small business websites, and they are often compared with each other. Both are run by companies headquartered in Osaka that combine off-the-shelf parts widely used by hosting companies overseas, allow adult sites, and leave referrals to A8.net affiliate programs. Laying the two dissections on top of each other, the difference between the companies shows less in the performance of their parts than in what they made standard on top of the same parts and what they moved to paid options or higher plans.

This article was put together by rereading both companies' official pricing, specification and feature pages, help centers, news and company profiles on October 11, 2026. This site has not signed up for both services under the same conditions to measure page speed or stability, so it does not rank how they feel in use. All prices include tax and are monthly equivalents when the full contract length is paid up front.

## Company and scale: two companies headquartered in Osaka

:::fact
According to the company profile of AzPocket, Inc. (as of 2026-10-13), the company that runs mixhost was founded on April 11, 2016, is headquartered in Minamisenba, Chuo Ward, Osaka, is a notified telecommunications carrier (E-28-03926), and names cPanel, L.L.C., LiteSpeed Technologies Inc. and Softaculous Ltd. as certified partners. The mixhost home page (as of 2026-10-11) says it is used by more than one million sites, from individuals to large companies. According to ColorfulBox's company information page (as of 2026-10-11), ColorfulLab, Inc., which runs ColorfulBox, was founded on January 23, 2018, is headquartered in Awaza, Nishi Ward, Osaka, has capital of 3 million yen, is a notified telecommunications carrier (E-30-04229), and launched the "ColorfulBox" hosting service in July 2018. It puts cumulative users at more than 24,000 (as of September 2026).
:::

| Item | ColorfulBox | mixhost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operator | ColorfulLab, Inc. (Nishi Ward, Osaka) | AzPocket, Inc. (Chuo Ward, Osaka) |

One published figure counts users and the other counts sites, so the two cannot be compared for size.

The parts lists are nearly identical. The difference is what each gives everyone and what each moves to higher plans, on top of the same parts.

## The parts list: assembled from the same off-the-shelf components

:::fact
According to ColorfulBox's specifications page (checked 2026-10-11), the web server is LiteSpeed 6.x.x, the OS is CloudLinux, the database is MariaDB 10.6.x, PHP runs from the 5.3 to the 8.3 series, the servers have Intel Xeon / AMD EPYC CPUs with up to 64 cores and up to 512 GB of memory, and storage is a RAID array of fast pure SSDs. The features page says cPanel is the control panel and Imunify360 provides security, and the FAQ on the home page says WAF / IPS / IDS through Imunify360 is standard on every plan. mixhost's pricing and feature comparison table (checked the same day) marks LiteSpeed Web Server, HTTP/3, CloudLinux and cPanel on every plan and lists MariaDB as the database and PHP 5.6 to 8.5. The same page explains that it uses cloud servers multiplexed across several servers and replicates its NVMe SSD storage in real time across several storage servers. A mixhost news post (2026-09-07) announced that Imunify360 had been introduced on the Light plan as well. This site's own observation (2026-10-11) found mixhost.jp and www.colorfulbox.jp both returning server: cloudflare, and the customers' login entry points, accounts.mixhost.jp and secure.colorfulbox.jp, both redirecting to clientarea.php and returning a cookie whose name starts with WHMCS.
:::

| Item | ColorfulBox | mixhost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web server | LiteSpeed (6.x) | LiteSpeed |
| OS | CloudLinux | CloudLinux |
| Control panel | cPanel | cPanel (WP Toolkit) |
| Security | Imunify360 (standard on every plan) | Imunify360 (added to Light in September 2026) |
| Database | MariaDB 10.6 | MariaDB |
| PHP | 5.3 to 8.3 | 5.6 to 8.5 |
| Storage | RAID array of pure SSDs | NVMe SSDs replicated in real time across several storage servers |
| Where servers sit | East or West Japan, chosen by the customer (East by default) | Tokyo |

:::guess
Neither company appears to build its own control panel, web server, security or billing; both assemble them from licensed products. That makes it possible to offer a range of features close to large rivals, but makes it hard to stand out on the performance of the parts. What the two companies can decide for themselves, then, is design: where to put servers, how far to extend backups, and which guarantee to give to whom. ColorfulBox reads as having invested in keeping servers in two regions, mixhost in multiplexing them as a cloud within one region.
:::

## Pricing: halve the first term, or keep the gap to renewal small

:::fact
According to ColorfulBox's pricing page (checked 2026-10-11), the monthly price on a 36-month contract is 528 yen for BOX1 (no discount; the home page gives 528 yen on renewal too), 484 yen for the first term and 968 yen on renewal for BOX2, 814 and 1,628 yen for BOX3, and 1,089 and 2,178 yen for BOX4; BOX2 and above have two tiers under a "first-term-only discount": a 50%-off first term and the renewal price. The first-term discount applies to 12-, 24- and 36-month contracts; 6-, 3- and 1-month contracts have a single price. Contract lengths are 1, 3, 6, 12, 24 or 36 months, there is no setup fee on contracts of three months or longer, and 2,200 yen on one-month contracts only. According to mixhost's price table (checked the same day), the monthly price on a 36-month contract is 495 yen for the first term and 748 yen on renewal for Light, 858 and 1,298 yen for Standard, 968 and 1,518 yen for Premium, and 1,408 and 2,178 yen for Business, with first-term discounts of 33% to 36% on 36 months. Contract lengths are 3, 6, 12, 24 or 36 months, and the discount applies only to the first payment period. According to a news post of April 1, 2025, mixhost cut renewal prices from April 22 that year; on 36-month contracts, Standard went from 2,178 to 1,298 yen, Premium from 3,278 to 1,518 yen and Business from 5,478 to 2,178 yen.
:::

| --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest plan | BOX1: 528 yen first term, 528 yen renewal | Light: 495 yen first term, 748 yen renewal |
| Second | BOX2: 484 yen first term, 968 yen renewal | Standard: 858 yen first term, 1,298 yen renewal |
| Third | BOX3: 814 yen first term, 1,628 yen renewal | Premium: 968 yen first term, 1,518 yen renewal |

| Contract lengths | 1, 3, 6, 12, 24 or 36 months | 3, 6, 12, 24 or 36 months |

The two companies describe what a plan contains in different terms. ColorfulBox uses SSD capacity, vCPUs and memory: BOX1 is 200 GB, 1 vCPU and 2 GB, BOX2 is 700 GB, 6 vCPUs and 8 GB. mixhost uses disk capacity, recommended monthly page views and a file count (inodes): Light is 100 GB, 100,000 page views and 100,000 files, Standard is unlimited disk, 200,000 page views and 200,000 files. The "second plan" on each side does not contain the same thing, so the table above is best read as each company's price list placed side by side in its own order.

:::guess
ColorfulBox prices only its cheapest plan, BOX1, at the renewal price from the start, and halves the first term on BOX2 and above before doubling back at renewal. It appears to be the common Japanese shared-hosting pattern, win on the entry price and recover from year two, made easier to grasp by keeping the multiple uniform. mixhost keeps its first-term discount in the 30% range and, by cutting renewal prices in 2025, narrowed the gap between the first term and renewal to about 1.5 to 1.6 times. It reads as a choice of a shape that lets it tell long-term customers, and the affiliate writers who recommend it to them, that renewal will not jump much. A customer who plans to move after three years benefits more from the cheap first term, and one who plans to stay five years or longer from the renewal price, so which is cheaper depends on how long it is used.
:::

## Backups: a separate region even on the cheapest plan, or a separate data center for all but Light

:::fact
According to ColorfulBox's features page (checked 2026-10-11), "region-separated automatic backup" keeps 14 days of automatic backups in a region separate from the one chosen at sign-up; the diagrams on the same page show data stored in East Japan backed up to West Japan, and data in West Japan backed up to East Japan. It is standard and free on every plan. The home page says the backup destination is set not on the same server but in a physically distant, separate region. The pricing page says the server's region can be chosen between East and West Japan (East by default). According to mixhost's pricing and feature comparison table (checked the same day), automatic backups copy files and databases once a day to an external backup server, Standard and above keep 14 days of them, and Light has none. Backups are stored redundantly on several storage servers in a separate data center, on storage described as having eleven nines (99.999999999%) of durability. The servers' region is Tokyo.
:::

| Item | ColorfulBox | mixhost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plans with automatic backups | every plan (including the cheapest, BOX1) | Standard and above (none on Light) |
| Period kept | 14 days | 14 days |
| Where they are stored | the other region (West Japan for East, East Japan for West) | several storage servers in a separate data center |
| Extra charge | none | none (included in the price of Standard and above) |

:::guess
Both have the same protection, a copy elsewhere if a server breaks, but extend it to different plans. ColorfulBox appears to keep it on its cheapest plan because region-separated backup is its banner. mixhost positions Light as an "eco plan for those who want to run a website at low cost," and reads as having created the 495-yen entry price by leaving backups out. Anyone choosing Light should count the effort of making their own backups as part of the price.
:::

## Trials and refunds: try before paying, or get money back after paying

:::fact
According to ColorfulBox's pricing page (checked 2026-10-11), every plan has a 30-day free trial from the day of application that can be used without a credit card. Signing a contract during the trial does not shorten it: the contract starts after the 30 days end. According to its notice under the Specified Commercial Transactions Act, cancellation of an order is accepted only within eight days of ordering, whether paid by card or bank transfer; fees for domains already registered cannot be cancelled or refunded, and whether to refund is decided at the company's discretion. According to mixhost's refund guarantee help page (checked the same day), web hosting, adult hosting and overseas hosting carry a 30-day refund guarantee that applies within 30 days of the first application and not after 30 days or at renewal. Refunds are not made automatically unless the customer chooses "cancel immediately" in the cancellation process and then contacts support. Domains, dedicated cloud hosting and some other services are excluded; bank transfer refunds carry a 2,200-yen fee, while card and PayPal refunds carry none.
:::

| Item | ColorfulBox | mixhost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Try before paying | 30-day free trial (no card needed) | none |
| Get money back after paying | cancellation within eight days of ordering (refund at the company's discretion) | 30-day refund guarantee (contact support after cancelling) |
| Excluded | fees for domains already registered | domains, dedicated cloud and others |

:::guess
Both use "30 days" as reassurance at the door, but point it in opposite directions. ColorfulBox hands over 30 days before payment, and the way out after paying is a short eight days. mixhost takes payment first and refunds within 30 days if it does not suit. The former appears built for people who want to try before deciding, the latter for people who want to sign up and start a production migration right away. In both cases, refunds and cancellations only start when the customer acts.
:::

## Uptime guarantees: an SLA on every plan, or only on the business tier

:::fact
mixhost's pricing and feature comparison table (checked 2026-10-11) marks a "99.99% uptime guarantee" on every plan. According to the SLA help page, the guarantee covers web hosting and cloud hosting, counts time when the www and mail services are completely unavailable as downtime, and refunds 10% of the monthly fee when monthly uptime falls below 99.99% but stays at or above 99.50%, and 30% below 99.50%. Claims must be made by the end of the month after the outage, and refunds are given as account credit or points applied to the next renewal or similar. The FAQ on ColorfulBox's home page (checked the same day) lists an SLA guaranteeing at least 99.99% uptime as one of the features that the business tier "Colorful Business" has on every plan, in addition to the base it shares with ColorfulBox. According to the business page, Colorful Business automatically grants account credit, with no claim needed, in a month that falls below the threshold, and prices start at 2,280 yen a month for Starter on a 36-month contract.
:::

| Item | ColorfulBox | mixhost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 99.99% uptime SLA | the Colorful Business tier (from 2,280 yen a month on a 36-month contract) | every plan (including Light) |
| When uptime falls short | account credit granted automatically (no claim needed) | 10% or 30% of the monthly fee returned as credit or points (claim by the end of the following month) |

:::guess
Where each company puts its SLA appears to reflect what it wants to sell to whom. mixhost attaches an uptime guarantee even to the cheap Light plan, promising individuals too that it will pay back if the service stops. ColorfulBox bundles its uptime guarantee with dedicated IP addresses, SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and .co.jp domains into a product for companies, and on its BOX plans for individuals makes region-separated backup the banner of protection. It reads as a difference in what customers are asked to pay for: not stopping, or being able to recover when something breaks.
:::

## Adult sites: allowed on the same price list, or split onto separate servers and IP addresses

:::fact
mixhost's pricing and feature comparison table (checked 2026-10-11) marks "adult sites" on every regular web hosting plan. Its adult hosting page carries the same price table as regular web hosting and says that, unless ordered by a court, it will not disclose a customer's personal information without consent, even in response to inquiries from investigative authorities or requests from third parties. According to ColorfulBox's features page (checked the same day), adult sites are hosted on servers separate from the regular ones, so an ordinary website never shares an IP address with an adult site. According to the pricing page, the adult servers come in BOX2 to BOX8; a 36-month BOX2 costs 528 yen for the first term and 1,320 yen on renewal (60% off the first term), and plans cannot be moved between the adult and regular servers. The adult hosting page says BOX2 and above include the Premium CDN.
:::

:::guess
mixhost treats adult sites as an extension of its regular plans and accepts them with privacy protection in front. ColorfulBox appears to separate servers and IP addresses to avoid the effect that housing ordinary and adult sites on the same server can have on IP reputation and mail delivery, and to put that cost, CDN included, into a higher renewal price. Seen from someone running an adult site, the difference is between mixhost, where one can start easily at the same price, and ColorfulBox, where one pays for an environment kept apart from ordinary sites.
:::

## Referral programs: publish the reward table, or two ASPs and friend invitations

:::fact
According to ColorfulBox's affiliate page (checked 2026-10-11), its affiliate program is offered through A8.net, participation is free with no quota, and the reward table is public. Rewards (before tax) include 1 yen for a 30-day free trial sign-up, 7,000 yen for a 36-month BOX1 contract and 8,000 yen for a 36-month BOX2 contract. According to mixhost's affiliate page (checked the same day), its ads are distributed through two affiliate service providers (ASPs), A8.net and afb, and the official page does not state reward amounts. According to its refer-a-friend page, a customer shares a dedicated invitation link and coupon code; the invitee who signs up with them gets a 1,000-yen discount, and once 30 days of use are confirmed the referrer receives 1,000 yen as account credit. There is no limit on the number of invitations.
:::

| Item | ColorfulBox | mixhost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affiliate program | A8.net (reward table published officially) | A8.net and afb (reward amounts not stated on the official page) |
| Example | 8,000 yen (before tax) for a 36-month BOX2, 1 yen for a free trial | — |
| Referrals by customers | — | 1,000 yen of credit for the referrer, a 1,000-yen discount for the invitee |

:::guess
Both companies appear to lean on affiliate programs that pay only on a sale rather than paying for advertising up front. ColorfulBox paying even 1 yen for a free-trial sign-up meshes with a design that makes the card-free trial the front door. mixhost keeps its rewards inside the ASPs while also paying 1,000 yen each way for customer-to-customer referrals, which reads as making word of mouth from current users another channel. When reading a recommendation of either, it is worth assuming the writer may be paid and checking the renewal price, not only the first-term price.
:::

## Eight shared technologies

The tech stack comparison shown below this page mechanically matches the techStack of the two articles. The technologies judged to be shared are eight, Cloudflare, CloudLinux, cPanel, Imunify360, LiteSpeed Web Server, MariaDB, PHP and WHMCS, the most of any comparison on this site (the previous high was four). Technologies that appear on only one side also reflect what each article happened to cover. Sitejet, for example, appears only on the ColorfulBox side, but mixhost's pricing and feature comparison table also marks Sitejet on every plan. LiteSpeed Cache appears only on the mixhost side, but ColorfulBox's features page also says LiteSpeed Cache comes as standard. Among the technologies that appear on one side only, the ones each company promotes as its own feature are ColorfulBox CDN and RocketBooster, a WordPress acceleration technology that mixhost says it developed itself.

The question for the buyer splits by what they want to pay for. If you want backups even on the cheapest plan, want to try for 30 days without registering a card before deciding, want to choose between East and West Japan for your server, or want a company package that bundles a dedicated IP address and a .co.jp domain, ColorfulBox fits. If you want an uptime guarantee even on a cheap plan, want to avoid a large jump at renewal, want to use newer PHP right away, or want a refund within 30 days after paying, mixhost fits. What separates two servers built from the same parts is not speed so much as how they decide who bears what when something breaks, stops, or does not suit.
