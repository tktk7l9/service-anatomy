When someone in Japan starts a blog or a small-business site on WordPress, the two names most likely to survive the shortlist are [ConoHa WING](/en/articles/conoha-wing) and [Xserver](/en/articles/xserver). Both are shared hosting — what Japan calls a "rental server," where many customers share one machine — and on the entry plans the officially published storage, CPU and memory figures are almost identical. Where they differ is in how the price is presented and how the contract binds you.

This piece is written only from what the two companies' official pages said when fetched on October 1, 2026. This site did not sign up for either service or measure its speed or usability. Prices and campaigns change on a short cycle, so check the official pricing pages for the latest numbers before signing up. All prices below are in Japanese yen and tax-inclusive (Japan's consumption tax), as both companies state.

## Comparing the prices — the same 36 months gets cheap in two different ways

:::fact
ConoHa WING's pricing page (as of October 1, 2026) says all displayed prices include tax and that the setup fee is free. There are two pricing types: "WING Pack," where you prepay the whole contract term in one payment, and "regular pricing" (通常料金), billed by the hour. Regular pricing is ¥2.5 per hour up to ¥1,452 per month for Basic, ¥4.4 up to ¥2,640 for Standard, and ¥8.8 up to ¥5,280 for Premium. WING Pack monthly prices are in the table below; the page's footnote calls them "campaign prices applied to new sign-ups made by 16:00 on October 7, 2026." The campaign is named the "EarlyAutumn campaign."

| ConoHa WING, WING Pack (tax incl., per month) | Basic | Standard | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 months | ¥1,331 | ¥2,530 | ¥5,060 |
| 6 months | ¥1,210 | ¥2,365 | ¥4,730 |
| 12 months | ¥991 | ¥2,145 | ¥4,290 |
| 24 months | ¥844 | ¥2,035 | ¥4,070 |
| 36 months | ¥649 | ¥1,925 | ¥3,850 |
:::

:::fact
Xserver's pricing page (as of October 1, 2026) likewise says all displayed prices include tax, with a setup fee of ¥0. The monthly figure is the contract-term price converted to a per-month amount, and payment is a single prepayment for the whole term. The page carries the note "campaign prices from 2026/9/7 17:00 to 2026/10/5 17:00 are applied," and new contracts of 12 months or longer get half of the fee back as cashback. The figures in parentheses are the monthly amounts the company presents as "effective" after cashback.

| Xserver (tax incl., per month) | Standard | Premium | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 months | ¥1,320 | ¥2,640 | ¥5,280 |
| 6 months | ¥1,210 | ¥2,420 | ¥4,840 |
| 12 months | ¥1,100 (effective ¥550) | ¥2,200 (effective ¥1,100) | ¥4,400 (effective ¥2,200) |
| 24 months | ¥1,045 (effective ¥522) | ¥2,090 (effective ¥1,045) | ¥4,180 (effective ¥2,090) |
| 36 months | ¥990 (effective ¥495) | ¥1,980 (effective ¥990) | ¥3,960 (effective ¥1,980) |
:::

The two tables look alike, but the mechanism differs. ConoHa WING's ¥649 is a price already discounted at the moment you sign up. Xserver's ¥495 is a conversion: you first prepay 36 months at ¥990, and the figure assumes you later receive half of it back.

:::fact
According to Xserver's campaign page, the cashback is not paid automatically. Around the 10th of the month after six full months have passed from the sign-up date, an email explaining how to apply is sent to the registered address, and you apply by following it. It is paid by bank transfer or at a convenience store (Seven Bank ATM or Lawson). If the server contract has already ended at the time of application — through a refund or cancellation, for example — it is not eligible. The campaign covers server accounts newly signed up for during the campaign period.
:::

By this site's simple arithmetic, the total paid up front on a 36-month contract is ¥649 × 36 = ¥23,364 for ConoHa WING Basic and ¥990 × 36 = ¥35,640 for Xserver Standard. With Xserver, ¥17,820 of that comes back more than half a year later. The amount that leaves your pocket first, and the need to remember to apply, are two differences the headline monthly price does not show.

### The price when you renew

:::fact
ConoHa WING's FAQ says of the ¥649 monthly price that "this campaign price is applied to the initial contract, and its content and discount rate may change or end." WING Pack auto-renewal is "ON" by default; the renewal payment is taken 30 days before the contract expiry date and cannot be cancelled after that. Xserver announced on October 31, 2025 that it would unify monthly prices that had differed between auto-renewal, manual renewal and new contracts, and said the monthly prices of 12-, 24- and 36-month contracts were unchanged. The half-price cashback covers new sign-ups.
:::

The specific monthly price applied when a ConoHa WING contract renews was not written as a number on the official pages this site checked. For Xserver, going by the official explanation, a renewal is billed at the price-table monthly rate without cashback (¥990 for Standard on 36 months). In neither case is the low price of the first term certain to continue. If you plan to stay for years, confirm with the company what you will pay after renewal rather than the first-term figure.

## What the entry plans contain — matching numbers, different words

:::fact
ConoHa WING Basic comes with 500 GB of SSD storage, 6 vCPU cores and 8 GB of memory. A footnote on the pricing page says "memory and vCPU core counts are reference values; when the shared server is not congested, you can use up to the maximum." Xserver Standard comes with 500 GB of NVMe disk. The "resource guarantee" entry in its specifications lists 6 virtual cores and 8 GB of memory for Standard on servers from sv13001.xserver.jp onward, and says data transfer is unlimited (with possible restrictions when the load is excessive).
:::

:::guess
The core and memory numbers are the same, but ConoHa WING presents them as "reference values" and Xserver as a "guarantee." The same 6 cores and 8 GB could mean different things depending on whether it is a ceiling you reach when the server is quiet or a share reserved for you. Both are still shared servers where many customers split one machine, and the official pages do not let us judge how this difference in wording shows up in actual page speed. Since ConoHa WING also has a separate "Business plan" that guarantees memory and vCPU, the company appears to treat guaranteed resources as a higher-tier product.
:::

### Speed and uptime are figures "the company states"

:::fact
ConoHa WING's top page displays "No. 1 in server processing speed" and "uptime of 99.99% or higher." Per its footnotes, the speed claim is the company's own survey from July 2026: it compared the average of five runs of h2load and Apache Bench against the lowest plans of the top ten services that together hold more than 90% of the domestic market. The uptime figure is the actual result from July 1, 2025 to July 31, 2026 "when an outage is defined as a state in which the server is completely inaccessible." Xserver's top page also displays "No. 1 in server speed" and "a track record of 99.99% or higher uptime," and says its server uptime has been 99.99% or higher since its founding.
:::

Since each company states that its own speed ranks first, these displays alone cannot settle which is faster. Both are the companies' claims, not measurements by this site. The footnote text giving the basis for Xserver's speed claim could not be confirmed within the top page this site fetched.

## Free domains, backups and WordPress tools

:::fact
Both companies offer up to two free custom domains with no renewal fee while the contract lasts. The conditions differ. At ConoHa WING only WING Pack customers qualify; regular pricing does not include it. The first domain is chosen from 20 types such as ".com" and ".net," and the second from eight: .online / .space / .website / .tech / .site / .fun / .tokyo / .shop. According to the FAQ, the domain cannot be changed to a different one once set, and if you cancel WING Pack or switch to regular pricing, renewal fees are charged from the following year. At Xserver, for new contracts, Standard gets one domain on a 12-month contract and two on 24 months or longer, while Premium and Business get two on any contract. Standard contracts shorter than 12 months are not eligible. Standard can choose from 11 types — .com / .net / .org / .info / .biz / .xyz / .link / .click / .blog / .online / .site — and the second domain from .blog / .site / .online. The campaign page also says that using this perk on Standard requires enabling the "auto-renewal setting."
:::

:::fact
Both companies describe backups as automatic and free on every plan, with the same retention: the past 14 days. ConoHa WING backs up websites, mail and databases once a day, and you can restore from the control panel. Xserver likewise copies web and mail data and MySQL databases to a dedicated backup server once a day, and you can restore from the server panel. Xserver states that this "does not guarantee the reliable preservation of data" and strongly recommends that customers also keep their own backups. On servers up to sv12301.xserver.jp, web and mail data is kept for the past 7 days.
:::

:::fact
The tools for starting WordPress also line up. ConoHa WING's "WordPress Kantan Setup" ("kantan" means easy) lets you apply for the server, a custom domain, WordPress, a WordPress theme and SSL together and configures them automatically; it is available when you sign up for WING Pack for the first time. For moving from another host there is "WordPress Kantan Migration," which the company says is free with no limit on migration data size and lets you check the site on a test site before the real move. Xserver has "WordPress Quick Start," which installs WordPress at the moment of sign-up, and "WordPress Easy Migration," used from the server panel.
:::

## Trials, cancellation and support — where the gap is widest

:::fact
The way you try each service differs. Xserver offers a 10-day free trial on every plan; paying during that period moves you to a full contract. During the trial, creating mail accounts, sending and receiving mail from programs, and creating additional FTP accounts are among the restricted functions. However, if you sign up using WordPress Quick Start there is no free trial, and payment is due at the same time as the application. According to the FAQ, cancelling costs nothing, but fees already paid are not refunded.
:::

:::fact
ConoHa WING's official pages do not show a free-trial scheme. Instead there are two mechanisms. Regular pricing has no setup fee and no minimum term; deleting the server at any time cancels the service, with no extra charge. WING Pack allows neither mid-term cancellation nor refunds. To end it, you switch auto-renewal "OFF" at least 30 days before the contract expiry date. A WING Pack contract term begins the month after you sign up, and usage until then is free, so signing up at the start of a month gives up to 31 days free. You can switch from regular pricing to WING Pack from the control panel whenever you like.
:::

:::fact
For support, Xserver lists phone (weekdays 10:00–18:00), email (accepted 24 hours a day, 365 days a year) and chat (weekdays 10:00–18:00), noting that phone and chat inquiries may be redirected to email depending on the content. ConoHa WING's pricing page says "phone and email support: yes" for every plan. ConoHa WING's phone hours could not be confirmed within the pages this site fetched. For payment, ConoHa WING takes credit cards and "ConoHa Charge," a prepaid balance you top up by bank transfer, convenience store, Pay-easy, Amazon Pay, PayPal and so on. Xserver takes credit cards, bank transfer, Pay-easy, deferred payment (Paidy), convenience-store payment and prepaid payment.
:::

:::pull
Xserver lets you "touch it for 10 days before paying." ConoHa WING lets you "pay by the hour and leave anytime." With either one, once you enter a long prepaid term there is no way back mid-term.
:::

## Which one fits whom

This is not a ranking. Here is what the official conditions suggest for each situation.

- If you want to keep the first payment small, ConoHa WING's WING Pack tends to fit. The discount applies at sign-up, and nothing has to be claimed later.
- If you are confident you will remember to apply half a year later, and can think in post-refund terms, Xserver's cashback tends to fit. On a 36-month contract the effective monthly price the company presents is ¥495 for Standard.
- If you want to check the control panel and behavior before committing, Xserver has the 10-day free trial. Note that using WordPress Quick Start removes the trial.
- If you may use it only for days or weeks, or have not decided whether to continue, ConoHa WING has regular pricing: billed by the hour, with no minimum term. It does not come with a free domain.
- If you want a 12-month contract to see how things go, watch the number of free domains. Xserver Standard gives one on 12 months; ConoHa WING's WING Pack gives up to two regardless of term.
- If the second domain matters to you, the choices differ: eight types at ConoHa WING, three at Xserver, overlapping on .online and .site.
- If you want to ask questions by chat, Xserver is the one that officially lists a chat channel.

Whichever you choose, a 36-month prepayment does not come back if you stop partway. If this is your first site and you are not sure you will keep it up, it is safer to separate the nudge that "longer contracts mean a lower monthly price" from your own realistic odds of continuing.

### What might make someone switch

:::guess
Both companies provide tools for migrating WordPress from another host, so the effort of switching itself appears smaller than it used to be. The likely triggers are a renewal price higher than the first term, realizing that the free-domain conditions are tied to the type of contract, and dissatisfaction after an outage or a support exchange. The free domain deserves attention: ConoHa WING states that renewal fees apply from the following year once you leave WING Pack. Because the domain cost remains even after moving the server, the gain or loss from switching is probably not decided by the hosting fee alone.
:::

## What lies behind the difference — VPS-born pricing and a specialist that kept selling prepaid terms

:::fact
ConoHa WING launched on September 26, 2018, according to GMO Internet Group's press release. Its operator, GMO Internet, Inc., is listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime Market (securities code 4784). Xserver began offering shared hosting in July 2003, according to the history on its corporate site.
:::

:::guess
The shape of each price list appears explainable from these origins. As [the ConoHa WING dissection](/en/articles/conoha-wing) describes, "ConoHa" was originally a VPS brand, and hourly billing is a design carried over from it. The monthly cap of that hourly rate is set as the baseline, and WING Pack is shown as a discount rate off it. That is why "leave anytime" and "no mid-term cancellation" sit in the same price list. It is also a product sold inside a listed company's group alongside domains and an AI writing tool, and the pattern of stacking time-limited campaigns to gather new sign-ups is presumed to follow from that.
:::

:::guess
Xserver, as [its dissection](/en/articles/xserver) describes, is a company that has kept selling shared hosting for a long time. Its price list starts from a list price per contract term, and the discount is taken outside it as cashback without rewriting the list price. Together with the 2025 unification of monthly prices across auto-renewal, manual renewal and new contracts, the division appears to be: leave the list-price table alone, win new customers with campaigns, and retain them by steering toward long terms. Keeping the 10-day free trial also looks like the way a company without hourly billing offers "try before you pay."
:::

:::guess
In other words, this site reads the difference between the two as less about which is cheaper and more about where each put the freedom to leave. ConoHa WING places that freedom in a separate pricing type, regular pricing, and has you give it up in exchange for the discount. Xserver places it in the first 10 days, and then locks in with the prepaid term. Shared hosting is a business where the cost per contract falls as more customers stay longer, so both designs are presumed to point the same way: a wide entrance that leads to long-term contracts.
:::

ConoHa WING and Xserver have almost identical numbers on their entry plans. What differs is the shape of the contract: whether you take the discount up front or receive it later, and whether the freedom to try lives in hourly billing or in ten free days. The monthly figures may be different by next week. What changes less easily is the design behind the price lists — a company that grew out of a VPS, and one that has kept selling shared hosting.
